### Step 3: Make Your First Decisions (10 minutes)

Use the [DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md](DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md) to answer:

1. **Do we have executive sponsorship?**
   - Yes → Continue

2. **Who will be on the POC team?**
   - Need: 1-2 projects, 5-10 developers, 1 tech lead
   - Tip: Choose enthusiastic early adopters

3. **What's our POC timeline?**
   - Recommended: 2-4 weeks
   - Start date: [Your date]
   - Review date: [Your date]

4. **What's our budget?**
   - POC budget: $5K-10K
   - Approved? Yes/No

## 🆘 Common Questions

### "How long will this take?"
- **POC**: 2-4 weeks
- **Full rollout**: 5-7 months
- **Enterprise maturity**: 12-18 months

### "How much will it cost?"
- **Year 1 total**: $325K-465K
- **ROI**: 140%-290% in Year 1
- **Per analysis**: $0.01-0.10

### "Do we need to do all 5 phases?"
- **Small org (<50 projects)**: Phases 1, 2, 4 may be sufficient
- **Medium org (50-500 projects)**: All phases recommended
- **Large org (500+ projects)**: All phases essential

### "Can we skip the POC?"
- **Not recommended**: POC validates feasibility and builds confidence
- **Alternative**: Combine POC + Pilot into extended pilot (6-8 weeks)

### "What if POC fails?"
- Document learnings
- Decide: Fix issues and retry, or table project
- No shame in "fail fast" - better to learn early

### "Who should lead this?"
- **Ideal**: Senior DevOps Engineer or Engineering Manager
- **Backup**: Tech Lead with strong Azure/AWS experience
- **Support**: Product Manager for stakeholder management
